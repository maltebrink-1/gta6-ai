Shorts uploader

This tool sends the short videos I make to my TikTok account @viceclips as drafts. I then review and post them myself in the TikTok app.

Step 1 — Connect TikTok

Log in with TikTok and allow: your basic profile, uploading videos to your drafts, and reading your account and video statistics.

Step 2 — Videos arrive in your TikTok drafts

After you approve a video in the review queue, it is uploaded to your TikTok inbox. Open TikTok → notifications/drafts → edit → Post.
