Privacy Policy

Shorts uploader — last updated 19 November 2026

Shorts uploader is a personal tool run by the owner of the TikTok account @viceclips and the YouTube channel MAL games. It uploads short videos that the owner creates to the owner's own accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What data we access

  • TikTok: basic profile information (display name, open ID), account statistics (follower and like counts) and the list of the account's videos with their view, like and comment counts. The tool also uploads videos to the account's drafts.
  • YouTube: the channel's statistics and the ability to upload videos to the owner's channel.

How we use it

Only to upload the owner's own videos and to show the owner's channel statistics on a private-use performance dashboard. Data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Where it is stored

Access tokens are stored as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets. Aggregated statistics (counts only) are stored in the project's GitHub repository. No data about other TikTok or YouTube users is collected.

Your choices

Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions) or in your Google Account (Security → Third-party connections).
